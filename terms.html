Terms of Use

The rules for using the Kinetix FSL app and its community. Please read them before you install the app.

Using Kinetix FSL

Kinetix FSL helps you learn Filipino Sign Language. It is a learning tool, not a certified interpreter, and the camera feedback can be wrong. The app is part of a student project and may change, stop working or be updated at any time. New versions come out on this website and are installed only when you accept them (Settings, Check for new updates). Install Kinetix FSL only from this website or from inside the app; a copy from anywhere else may have been changed and may not be safe.

Your account

Give true information, keep your password to yourself, and tell us if someone else uses your account. You must be at least 13 to make an account (see the Privacy Notice for 13 to 17 year olds). You are responsible for what happens under your account. If you sign up with an email and password, confirm your email address before you post, comment, chat or follow people; you can learn in the meantime.

Community rules

Be kind. Do not harass, threaten or shame anyone. Do not post other people’s private information, sexual or violent content, or anything illegal. Do not pretend to be someone else, including the app itself. Do not spam. Do not try to break or misuse the app.

Your content

What you post stays yours. By posting it you let the app show it to other users for as long as it is posted. Only post what you have the right to share. You can delete your posts and your account at any time.

Reports and moderation

Anyone can report a post, comment or chat. Moderators and administrators may remove content, warn you, limit what your account can do for a time, or disable the account when the rules are broken. If you think a decision is wrong, write to privacy@kinetixfsl.app.

No guarantees

The app is provided as it is, during a pilot and evaluation period. We try to keep it working and safe, but we cannot promise it will always be available, correct or free of errors. As far as the law allows, the team is not responsible for losses that come from using the app.

Changes and contact

These terms have a version number and may change; the new version is in the app (Settings) and on this website. Questions: privacy@kinetixfsl.app.
